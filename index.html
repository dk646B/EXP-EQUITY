Process

How EXP EQUITY works

Every gig moves through a locked pipeline — from verified onboarding to direct vendor payment, with no cash leakage.

01

Sponsor Posts a Gig

Sponsor defines a clear Definition of Done (DoD) checklist and sets a flat fee. Platform locks funds into escrow before any work begins.

02

Graduate Accepts

A verified contractor reviews the DoD and accepts. Project status shifts to Active. Scope is now legally frozen — no hidden requests.

03

Work & Submit

Graduate delivers the work via the platform. Sponsor has 72 hours to review against the checklist. No response = auto-approval.

04

Escrow Releases to Wallet

Approved funds go to the graduate's Restricted Wallet — a programmable ledger that routes payments directly to verified vendors.

05

Bills Get Paid, Portfolio Grows

Rent, utilities, and groceries are paid directly to vendors. The completed project is added to the graduate's verified portfolio.

Escrow Pipeline

Every dollar is tracked
from post to payment

1

Sponsor funds escrow

Payment is charged via Stripe and held in the EXP EQUITY escrow account. No funds are released until a verifiable outcome is reached.

PENDING → ACTIVE
2

Contractor submits deliverables

Work is uploaded to the platform. Sponsor has 72 hours to review against the locked DoD checklist. Silence = approval.

ACTIVE → SUBMITTED
3

Escrow releases to wallet

Upon approval, funds move to the contractor's Restricted Digital Wallet. Maximum 2 revision cycles protect against scope creep.

SUBMITTED → PAID
4

Vendor payment + portfolio entry

Bills are paid directly to verified vendors. The project is anonymized and added to the contractor's verified public portfolio.
