The Wallet

Assurance for everyone

The Restricted Wallet isn't a limitation — it's the guarantee. Sponsors know exactly where their money goes. Contractors know their essentials are covered.

Only necessities. No exceptions.

Funds are locked to essential categories via Merchant Category Codes (MCCs) — the same classification system used by Visa and Mastercard. The wallet cannot process a transaction at a bar, a casino, or an electronics retailer. It can only pay for things you need to survive and keep working.

  • Housing & Rent — direct to property management companies and landlords via ACH
  • Utilities — electric, gas, water, and internet providers only
  • Groceries — verified physical and digital supermarkets, not convenience stores
  • Transportation — fuel stations and public transit authorities
What's blocked: Cash withdrawals, transfers to personal bank accounts, restaurants, retail, entertainment, and any non-necessity MCC. Violations trigger account review.
Escrow Pipeline

Every dollar is tracked
from post to payment

The money never sits loose. Here's exactly how funds move from sponsor charge to vendor payment — with full audit trail at every stage.

1

Sponsor charges escrow

Payment is charged via Stripe and held in the EXP EQUITY escrow account. No funds are released until a verifiable outcome is reached.

The full gig fee is charged upfront before the gig goes live on the board. Sponsors cannot post without funding. This ensures contractors never complete work for a sponsor who can't pay.
PENDING → ACTIVE
2

Contractor accepts & scope locks

Contractor accepts the gig. At this moment, the DoD checklist is cryptographically frozen — no additions, no modifications.

Both parties receive a timestamped copy of the locked scope. Any later request that falls outside the DoD is automatically out-of-scope and unenforceable on the platform.
ACTIVE → IN PROGRESS
3

Deliverables submitted

Work is uploaded to the platform. Sponsor receives a 72-hour review window to approve or open a dispute against the locked checklist.

Disputes are evaluated line-by-line against the original DoD — nothing subjective. If the sponsor does not respond within 72 hours, the system auto-approves and releases funds. This prevents contractors from being ghosted.
IN PROGRESS → SUBMITTED
4

Escrow releases to Restricted Wallet

Upon approval, funds move to the contractor's Restricted Digital Wallet. Maximum 2 revision cycles are permitted before auto-approval kicks in regardless.

The wallet balance is a ledger entry, not a bank balance. It can only be drawn against verified vendor accounts pre-registered in the contractor's profile. No transfers to unregistered accounts are possible.
SUBMITTED → PAID
5

Direct vendor payment + portfolio entry

Bills are paid directly to verified vendors via ACH or card. The project is anonymized and added to the contractor's verified public portfolio with the sponsor's endorsement.

The full audit trail — escrow charge, scope lock timestamp, submission, approval, and vendor payout — is logged and available to both parties permanently.
EQUITY CAPTURED

Dispute Resolution

  • Disputes are evaluated only against the locked DoD checklist
  • No subjective judgment — each checklist item is binary: met or not met
  • Partial approval is possible if some checklist items are complete
  • Platform admin arbitrates within 48 hours of a filed dispute
  • Repeated bad-faith disputes result in sponsor account suspension

Revision Policy

  • Maximum 2 revision cycles per gig — no unlimited loops
  • Revisions must be scoped to items explicitly listed in the original DoD
  • After 2 cycles, work auto-approves if sponsor hasn't resolved the dispute
  • Revision requests outside original scope are rejected by the platform

Wallet Security

  • Vendor accounts are pre-registered and verified before payout is possible
  • MCC restrictions are enforced at the transaction layer — not just the UI
  • All transactions generate receipts visible to both the contractor and admin
  • Wallet balance cannot be withdrawn as cash under any circumstances

About Us

Built for the gap nobody wanted to fix

EXP EQUITY started with a simple observation: the most motivated workers in the country — recent graduates — have no reliable bridge between school and stable employment.

Freelance platforms exploit them. Charities don't give them credentials. Corporate hiring ignores them until they have experience they can't get without a job.

We built a third option: a structured, accountable exchange where verified professionals earn real income that goes directly to verified necessity vendors. No middlemen. No cash that can evaporate. No scope disputes decided by whoever yells loudest.

The platform enforces fairness through architecture, not policy. Scope locks are technical. Wallet restrictions are enforced at the transaction layer. The rules don't bend because the system doesn't let them.

🏗️

Structure over trust

We don't ask sponsors to be fair. We build a system where being unfair isn't an option. Scope locks, escrow, and auto-approval are architecture — not promises.

📊

Accountability at every layer

Every dollar, every checklist item, every approval timestamp is logged. Neither party can rewrite history. The audit trail is permanent and shared.

🎓

Credentials, not charity

Contractors earn verified portfolio entries — not handouts. Sponsors get real work done — not donations. The exchange is mutual and on record.

🛡️

Dignity as a design principle

We built the wallet restriction not to control contractors, but to protect them — from financial mismanagement, from sponsor distrust, and from a system that defaults to assuming the worst.
