Sponsor FAQs

Common questions from Sponsors

Your payment method is not charged when you post a gig — it's only charged when a verified Graduate accepts and claims your gig. At that point, the platform automatically initiates an escrow charge via Stripe and holds the funds until the DoD is verified. If no one claims your gig within 7 days, it auto-expires and you are never charged.

Yes, before a Graduate claims the gig. Once a Graduate accepts and escrow locks (status changes to Active), the Definition of Done checklist is legally frozen and cannot be altered by either party. This protects both you and the Graduate from disputes over shifting requirements.

Once a Graduate submits their deliverables, you have exactly 72 hours to review and respond. If you do not approve, reject, or open a dispute within that window, the platform automatically approves the submission and releases escrow funds to the Graduate's Restricted Wallet. This is by design — it prevents Graduates from being held hostage by unresponsive sponsors. You should set a reminder as soon as you receive the submission notification.

A maximum of two (2) revision cycles. A valid revision request must cite specific DoD items that were not met — aesthetic preferences or requests that weren't in the original brief are flagged as unauthorized scope creep and rejected by the system. After two revision cycles, if you still believe the DoD was not met, you may open a formal dispute.

If the submitted work doesn't meet the DoD and revisions haven't resolved it, you can trigger a Disputed status within the 72-hour review window. This freezes the escrow account. You then have 48 hours to submit written documentation specifying exactly which DoD items were not fulfilled. The platform admin acts as a neutral arbitrator and rules based strictly on the original checklist — not subjective taste.

To open a dispute, use the contact form on this page or email the admin directly with your gig ID and the specific checklist items at issue.

You can cancel an unclaimed gig at any time with no charge — escrow hasn't been triggered yet. Once a Graduate claims the gig and escrow locks, cancellations require admin involvement. If cancellation is approved due to a legitimate reason, the full escrow amount is refunded to your original payment method. Contact us via the form on this page to initiate.

After 48 hours with no bids, the platform sends you an automated request to increase the project budget. Our algorithm benchmarks fair pricing by category — underpriced gigs receive significantly fewer bids. If the gig remains unclaimed for 7 consecutive days, it auto-expires and is archived. You can repost it with a higher compensation or adjusted scope at any time.

Your payment goes directly into the EXP EQUITY Escrow Account via Stripe — it never goes to the Graduate's personal bank account. Upon approval, funds transfer to the Graduate's Restricted Digital Wallet, which is locked to essential spending categories (rent, utilities, groceries, transport) via Merchant Category Codes. You can be confident the money is going toward survival costs, not arbitrary spending.
